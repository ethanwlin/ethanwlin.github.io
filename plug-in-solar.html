Notable Features

A 286Wh LiFePO4 battery paired with a 45W foldable solar panel, giving customers a self-contained unit that both generates and stores power rather than requiring a separate battery purchase. Rated for 600W of continuous output with X-Boost surge support up to 1200W, it can run most small household electronics and recharges fully from solar in roughly 1.5 hours with a full 220W solar input. Its compact, sub-11-lb design and 3,000-cycle lifespan make it a strong fit for customers who want an entry-level, highly portable option that doubles as backup power and an outdoor companion, with the flexibility to expand storage capacity later with an add-on battery.

Notable Features

A complete DIY solar kit including four 200W bifacial panels, two 400W smart inverters certified to UL 1741 and IEEE 1547, a Smart PowerMeter, and mounting hardware, all designed to be installed by the homeowner without a licensed electrician or utility interconnection agreement. The system connects to any standard outlet and can produce roughly 1,800–1,900 kWh per year, meaningfully offsetting always-on loads like refrigerators, routers, and lighting. It's a strong fit for customers who want a full, scalable plug-in system out of the box and are comfortable with a larger upfront investment in exchange for the highest output in this category.

Notable Features

A grid-tied, microinverter-only unit with no built-in battery, giving customers full flexibility to choose their own solar panels rather than being locked into a fixed kit. Dual independent MPPT channels support two panels per unit — recommended in the 315W to 660W range — and built-in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth connect directly to the APsystems app for real-time monitoring without an external gateway. It plugs into a standard outlet with the included power cord and is backed by a 12-year warranty once registered. This is the best option for customers who already own or plan to source their own panels and want the lowest-cost entry point into plug-in solar.
